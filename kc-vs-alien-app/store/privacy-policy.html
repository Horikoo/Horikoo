KC vs Alien Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

KC vs Alien is an offline arcade game. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score on your own device so it can show it next time you play. This number never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any special permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the email address shown on the app's store page.
